# General Settings

<nav to="general">

## Appearance

### Language
Choose the language of the app. The default follows your device. You can help translate the app on [Weblate](https://hosted.weblate.org/engage/ytdlnis/).

### Theme
`System`, `Dark` or `Light`.

### App Icon
Pick one of the alternative launcher icons. The app closes after the change and the icon might take a moment to update.

### Accents
Pick the accent color: Material You (dynamic colors from your wallpaper, on supported devices), Blue, Red, Green, Purple, Yellow, Orange or Monochrome.

### High contrast
A pure black / white theme.

### Hide thumbnails
Hide the thumbnails in `Home`, `Downloads` and the `Download queue`. Useful to make the lists more compact and to save data.

### Modify download card
Choose which elements are visible in the [Download Card](/docs/guides/download-card): Schedule, Title, Author, Container, Adjust audio, Adjust video, Adjust templates and URL.

### Display over apps
Make the download card always display on top. Turn this on if the currently running app closes when the download card shows up from the [Share Menu](/docs/guides/share-menu).

## YouTube

### Video recommendations
Fill the home screen with videos. See [Home](/docs/guides/home#video-recommendations). Depending on the source you can set an `API key` or a custom URL.

### Data fetching extractor (YouTube)
`yt-dlp` or `NewPipe`. See [YouTube](/docs/guides/youtube).

### Formats source
Where the formats of YouTube videos come from, `NewPipe` or `yt-dlp`.

### Use item URL instead of playlist URL
Playlist metadata won't be embedded with this enabled.

## Miscellaneous

### Quick Action Button
What the button on the cards of the Downloads screen does: `Share` or `Open File`.

### Preferred locale
The country used by NewPipe and the YouTube API to get trending / recommended videos.

### Preferred home screen
The screen that opens when you launch the app: Home, Downloads, Download queue or More.

### Navigation bar
Reorder and show or hide the items of the navigation bar.

### Label visibility
When the navigation bar labels show: `Always`, only on the `Selected` item or `Never`.

### Preferred search engine
The website used for searches typed in Home: YouTube, YouTube Music, SoundCloud, Bilibili, PRX Series, PRX Stories, Rokfin or Netverse.

### Check clipboard in the home screen
Automatically detect URLs in the clipboard in the home screen.

### Search suggestions
Get search suggestions from Google while typing.

### Swipe gestures
Choose where swiping an item does something (Downloads, In queue, Cancelled, Scheduled, Errored, Saved, Command templates, Cookies, Multiple download card).

### Swipe gestures in download card
Swipe horizontally in the download card to switch between audio, video and command.

### Show terminal in share menu
Adds the [Terminal](/docs/guides/terminal) as an option in the share sheet.

### Show option to download immediately in the share menu
Adds an option in the share sheet that starts the download immediately without showing the card.

### Show counts in the download queue screen
Shows the number of items in each tab of the [Download Queue](/docs/guides/download-queue).

### Ignore battery optimization
Opens the Android setting to exclude the app from battery optimization, so downloads and observe sources keep running in the background.

### Reset
Resets every preference in this screen.
